# Tasks & planner

Personal puts **Tasks** and **Planner** side by side: capture what needs doing, then shape the day.

| Sidebar label | Path | Role |
| ------------- | ---- | ---- |
| **Tasks** | `/app/tasks` | Unified (or Personal-only) task list |
| **Planner** | `/app/planner/plan` | Plan and replan the day |
| *(Today)* | `/app/planner/day` | Minimal schedule + due items |

## Guides

1. [Tasks](/personal/tasks-planner/tasks) — create, filter, and complete work across Personal and other workspaces
2. [Planner](/personal/tasks-planner/planner) — build today’s plan and open the day view

Business project delivery with phases and client linkage is covered under [Business](/work). Personal projects are life-sized — DIY, holidays, and personal plans — see [Projects & notes](/personal/projects-notes).
