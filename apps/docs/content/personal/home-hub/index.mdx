# Home hub

**Home** (`/app`) is the Personal hub: today at a glance, shortcuts into the surfaces you use most, and optional visibility into tasks and plans from other workspaces.

Personal stays a hub — not a CRM. Sales clients and invoicing belong in [Business](/work).

## Today

1. Open **Home**, or jump straight to the day view at `/app/planner/day`.
2. Review what is scheduled and due for today.
3. Open an item to complete it, reschedule it in **Planner** (`/app/planner/plan`), or capture a follow-up in **Tasks** (`/app/tasks`).
4. When the day needs a rethink, use Planner’s replan flow rather than hunting across workspaces.

## Shortcuts

Shortcuts are fast links on Home (and in mobile navigation) to Personal routes you care about.

1. Open **Personal settings** → shortcuts preferences (`/app/settings/shortcuts`), or manage them from the Home shortcut picker where available.
2. Choose destinations such as **Tasks**, **Planner**, **Projects**, **Notes**, **People**, **Personal email**, or **Today**.
3. Reorder or trim the list so the first screen stays calm — one job per shortcut.
4. On mobile, the bottom navigation reflects the shortcuts you keep.

Useful destination paths:

| Label | Path |
| ----- | ---- |
| Tasks | `/app/tasks` |
| Planner | `/app/planner/plan` |
| Today | `/app/planner/day` |
| Projects | `/app/projects` |
| Notes | `/app/notes` |
| People | `/app/people` |
| Personal email | `/app/email` |

## Cross-workspace visibility

Personal can surface work from Business and other memberships so you plan one day, not five apps.

1. Confirm you belong to at least one other workspace via the account switcher.
2. In Tasks preferences, include workspace tasks when you want a unified list (`/app/tasks` can filter by Personal only or by all workspaces).
3. Use Planner to schedule across Personal life and workspace delivery when your plan scope allows it.
4. Switch into Business only when you need CRM, invoices, or portals — then return to **Home** for the day view.

### What stays in Business

Clients, pipeline stages, invoicing, activity feeds, meetings workflows, and client portals are documented under [Business](/work). Personal may show related tasks; it does not replace those modules.
