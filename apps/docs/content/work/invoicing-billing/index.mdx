# Invoicing & billing

Two related ideas share the word “billing” in studios — keep them separate:

1. **Client invoices** — you bill your clients from Ozer (**Commercial → Invoices**).
2. **Workspace subscription** — you pay Ozer for the Business plan (**Settings → Billing**).

## Goal

Get paid by clients, and keep your Ozer plan and seats in good order.

## Prerequisites

- Business workspace with invoicing modules enabled for client billing
- Owner/admin access for subscription changes

## Guides in this section

- [Client invoices](/work/invoicing-billing/client-invoices) — create, send, track (plus proposals & contracts)
- [Workspace subscription](/work/invoicing-billing/workspace-subscription) — Solo / Team / Scale plans, add-ons, Stripe portal

## Where to click

| Job | Sidebar / settings | Path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoice clients | **Commercial → Invoices** | `/app/[account]/invoices` |
| Proposals | **Commercial → Proposals** | `/app/[account]/proposals` |
| Contracts | **Commercial → Contracts** | `/app/[account]/contracts` |
| Payment defaults | **Settings → Payments** | `/app/[account]/settings/payments` |
| Services catalogue | **Settings → Services** | `/app/[account]/settings/services` |
| Your Ozer plan | **Settings → Billing** | `/app/[account]/settings/billing` |
