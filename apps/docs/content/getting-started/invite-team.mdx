# Invite your team

On a **team workspace**, invite members from settings with a role that matches what they should access.

## Tips

- Start with owners/admins for setup, then invite collaborators
- Use a shared workspace for client work; keep personal CRM items in your personal account when that split helps
- Permissions follow workspace roles — RLS enforces access on the server

Detailed roles and permissions are expanding; if something looks wrong after an invite, have the member sign out and back in, then contact support.
