# People

**People** (`/app/people`) is your personal list of friends, family, and catch-ups — not a sales CRM.

Client companies, leads, and pipeline contacts live in [Business](/work).

## Open People

1. Select **People** in the Personal sidebar, or go to `/app/people`.
2. Browse the list and open someone to see their detail page (`/app/people/[personId]`).
3. Use the viewer context on the page to keep updates personal and private to your home.

## Add or update someone

1. From **People**, create a person with a name you will recognise.
2. Add the context that helps a future you — relationship notes, reminders, or catch-up history as the feature supports.
3. Save, then reopen the person from the list when you need them.
4. Promote anything that becomes a shared company relationship into Business clients or contacts instead of stretching People into CRM.

## How People fits Personal

1. Use People for life relationships and social follow-ups.
2. Capture “I should message X” as a **Task** (`/app/tasks`) when it needs a due date.
3. Keep sales opportunities and studio contacts in Business pipeline and clients.

## Tips

- Personal stays the free hub; People stays lightweight by design.
- Avoid duplicating Business CRM records here — switch workspace when you need pipeline.
- Pair People with **Notes** for longer context that does not belong on a contact card.
