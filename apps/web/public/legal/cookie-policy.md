# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 28 July 2026. Oodle Designs Ltd ("Ozer") uses cookies and similar technologies on ozer.so and related services. This policy explains what we use and how you can control them.

## Essential cookies

Required for sign-in, security (including CSRF protection), load balancing, and remembering your cookie preferences. These cannot be switched off if you use the product.

## Analytics cookies and similar technologies

With your consent we use **PostHog** (EU Cloud) for product analytics (page views, feature usage, diagnostics) and **session replay** (recordings retained for 30 days; input fields masked). PostHog may set cookies or use local/session storage and similar technologies to distinguish visits and support replay.

These technologies are only loaded after you accept analytics via the cookie banner (or an equivalent in-product preference control). You can withdraw consent at any time via the cookie banner or your browser settings; withdrawing consent does not delete historical analytics already processed.

Further detail on what PostHog receives is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) (Product analytics section).

## Preferences

We may store preferences such as theme (light/dark) and locale so the site remembers your choices between visits.

## Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in and core features from working. For privacy questions contact privacy@ozer.so.
