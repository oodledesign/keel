# Marketing copy style

Applies to every string in `lib/marketing/*.ts` and marketing page components.
`copy-style.test.ts` enforces the mechanical rules; the rest is on the writer.

## Rules

- **One em dash per paragraph, at most.** Use a full stop, a comma or "so". Page
  titles use `Page name | Ozer`.
- **Banned words:** "actually", "calm" (and "calmer", "calmly"), "one place",
  "seamless" / "seamlessly". Say what happens instead.
- **No "not X, it's Y" constructions.** State the thing directly.
- **No reflexive triples.** Only list three things when there are exactly three.
- **Specific over general.** Prices, seat counts, portal names and timings beat
  "powerful", "effortless" and "all-in-one".
- **UK English and UK trade terms.** For commercial property: instructions,
  disposals, applicants, requirements, heads of terms, particulars, under offer.
  For studios: clients, jobs, invoices, retainers.
- **Headlines are sentences.** Sentence case, a full stop when it's a statement,
  and no recoloured half-headline.
- **Sentence-case labels and buttons.** "Join the waiting list", not "Join The
  Waiting List".
