# HoTs, proposals & sales

Commercial deal paperwork and the completed register live in two places:

| Sidebar | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HoTs / Proposals | `/app/[account]/proposals` | Heads of terms, proposals, and deal paperwork in flight |
| Sales register | `/app/[account]/leases` | Completed lettings / sales outcomes |

## Create HoTs / a proposal

1. Open `/app/[account]/proposals`.
2. Choose **New** (or the create action on the page).
3. Link the relevant **disposal** and **contacts** (applicant, landlord, solicitor).
4. Capture headline commercial terms (rent / price, Lease length, incentives, conditions).
5. Save and share with the appropriate parties outside Ozer if needed (email / PDF).
6. Move related WIP to **Under Offer / Negotiating** on `/app/[account]/pipeline`.
7. Keep Interest status on the disposal aligned (offer / negotiating / under offer).

## Progress a deal to completion

1. Update HoTs as terms change — avoid parallel “final” documents with conflicting numbers.
2. When solicitors are instructed, note them on Contacts and on the proposal record.
3. On exchange / completion:
   1. Move WIP to **Completed / Exchanged**.
   2. Set the disposal status to **Let** or **Sold**.
   3. Mark related requirements **Fulfilled** when this search is done.
4. Add or update the row on the **Sales register** at `/app/[account]/leases`.

## Sales register

1. Open `/app/[account]/leases`.
2. Confirm completed deals appear with correct parties, dates, and disposal references.
3. Use register status (for example active / expired / terminated on lease-style rows) according to your office convention.
4. Cross-check Insights so completed volumes match what partners expect.

## When a deal falls away

1. Update or close the HoTs / proposal record.
2. Move WIP to **Fallen through**.
3. Revert Interest status (withdrawn / lost) on the disposal.
4. Leave the disposal **Marketing** or **Withdrawn** depending on whether stock is still available.
5. Keep Requirements on **Actively Searching** or **Withdrawn** as the applicant decides.
