# Getting started

Get from signup to a working commercial agency workspace in a few minutes.

## Steps

1. [Create your account](/commercial-property/getting-started/create-account)
2. [Set up your first commercial workspace](/commercial-property/getting-started/first-workspace)
3. [Invite teammates](/commercial-property/getting-started/invite-team) and choose billable vs support seats
4. Open the sidebar and confirm you can see Disposals, WIP, Contacts, and Requirements
5. Create a draft disposal on `/app/[account]/listings`, then explore [listing detail](/commercial-property/disposals/listing-detail)

## After setup

Once you are in:

1. Read [Workspace navigation](/commercial-property/workspace/navigation)
2. Work through [Disposals](/commercial-property/disposals) and [WIP](/commercial-property/wip-pipeline)
3. Configure [Website & portals](/commercial-property/publishing-portals) when credentials are ready
4. Review [Seats & billing](/commercial-property/seats-billing) before hiring extra negotiators
