# Portals & websites

Share selected work with clients through portals, and publish marketing sites from Ozer when you use the sites product.

## Client portals

Invite clients to a scoped view of projects, files, or status without giving them a full workspace login.

## Websites

Studio sites run on Ozer’s sites stack (custom domains such as `*.sites.ozer.so` or your own domain when configured).
