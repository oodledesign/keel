# Ozer Commercial property

Ozer Commercial property is the Workspace OS for commercial agency teams — disposals, WIP instructions, acquisition requirements, viewings, HoTs, and portal publishing in one place.

This documentation is written for UK commercial agents and office managers. App URLs use your workspace slug: `/app/[account]/…`.

## Who it’s for

- Sole practitioners and micro agencies running occupational and investment disposals
- Regional commercial desks that need shared WIP, requirements, and contacts
- Teams that publish to a Property Hive website and major UK portals
