# Planner

**Planner** (`/app/planner/plan`) shapes the day across Personal life and the workspaces in scope. The minimal **Today** view lives at `/app/planner/day`.

## Build a plan

1. Open **Planner** in the sidebar (`/app/planner/plan`).
2. Review available tasks from Personal and any workspaces included in your plan scope.
3. Place or adjust blocks for the day so the schedule matches energy and deadlines.
4. Save the plan — then check it on **Today** (`/app/planner/day`).

## Replan the day

1. When priorities shift, open the replan controls in Planner.
2. Note what changed (new urgency, blocked work, time lost).
3. Accept an updated plan and confirm it on `/app/planner/day`.
4. Carry unfinished items back into **Tasks** (`/app/tasks`) rather than leaving them only in memory.

## Day view

1. Open `/app/planner/day` (or a **Today** shortcut from Home).
2. Follow the schedule and tick due tasks as you go.
3. Jump back to `/app/planner/plan` when the structure needs another pass.

## Tips

- Set timezone and working hours in **Personal settings** so plans land on the right clock.
- Use Planner for one coherent day; use Business project boards for multi-week client delivery ([Business docs](/work)).
- Home hub shortcuts can pin **Planner** or **Today** — see [Home hub](/personal/home-hub).
