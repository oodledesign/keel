# Personal & team accounts

## Goal

Choose the right account type for data, sharing, and billing.

## Prerequisites

- An Ozer login ([Create your account](/work/getting-started/create-account))

## Account types

### Personal account

- Linked to your user ID (`auth.users.id` matches `accounts.id` in Makerkit terms).
- Good for private pipelines, personal projects, planner, and life context that isn’t shared with a studio.
- Reach it via `/app` and the personal entries in the account switcher.

### Team (Business) account

- A shared workspace with **members**, **roles**, and shared CRM / projects / invoices.
- Data is scoped by `account_id`. You only see what your membership allows.
- URLs use the workspace slug: `/app/[account]/…`.
- Billing for Business plans lives under **Settings → Billing** on that account.

## Steps: create another Business workspace

1. Open the account switcher in the sidebar.
2. Create a new team / Business workspace.
3. Name it, set the slug, and pick the Business profile.
4. Invite people from **Team** (`/app/[account]/members`) as needed.

Create additional team workspaces when you need a clean boundary between brands or legal entities.

## Tips

- Don’t put studio client data only in personal space if colleagues need access — use a Business workspace.
- One person can belong to many team accounts; switch deliberately before creating clients or invoices.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Created data in the wrong space | Switch account in the switcher; recreate or migrate by creating records in the correct workspace |
| Can’t access a colleague’s workspace | Ask an owner to invite your email under **Team** |
