# Projects & notes

Personal **Projects** and **Notes** keep life plans organised without turning Personal into a studio CRM.

| Sidebar label | Path | Role |
| ------------- | ---- | ---- |
| **Projects** | `/app/projects` | Personal projects — DIY, holidays, parties, plans |
| **Notes** | `/app/notes` | Markdown notes private to your home |

Client jobs, phases for paid work, and delivery boards belong in a [Business workspace](/work).

## Projects

1. Open **Projects** (`/app/projects`).
2. Create a project with a short name and enough context to recognise it later.
3. Add tasks or phases as needed for your plan — keep scope personal, not client CRM.
4. Open a project from the list to update status, tasks, and details.
5. Link related day work from **Tasks** (`/app/tasks`) or schedule focus time in **Planner** (`/app/planner/plan`).

### Good fits for Personal projects

- Home DIY and renovations
- Holidays and travel prep
- Parties, gifts, and life admin bundles
- Personal learning plans

### Better in Business

- Client retainers and delivery
- Pipeline-linked jobs
- Anything that needs invoices or a client portal

## Notes

1. Open **Notes** (`/app/notes`).
2. Create a note — content is saved as Markdown and stays private to Personal.
3. Organise with folders or categories when available.
4. Open `/app/notes/new` for a blank note, or an existing note at `/app/notes/[noteId]`.
5. Keep scrap thinking in Notes; promote action items into **Tasks** when they are due.

## Tips

- One purpose per project; park ideas in Notes until they earn a project.
- Personal project URLs sit under `/app/projects` with no account slug.
- Switch to Business when a plan becomes billable client work.
