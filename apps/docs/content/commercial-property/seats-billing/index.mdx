# Seats & billing

Commercial property uses **graduated per-seat billing** for **billable** seats, plus a small number of free **support** seats at team scale.

Manage membership on **Team** (`/app/[account]/members`) and the plan under workspace billing settings.

## Billable vs support

| Seat kind | Pays on Stripe | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Billable** | Yes | Create/edit disposals, move WIP and requirement stages, configure Website & portals, run day-to-day negotiating work |
| **Support** | No (within free allowance) | View workspace data and contribute notes / activity; **cannot** mutate stages, disposals, or publishing |

If a support user tries a restricted action, Ozer asks them to use a billable colleague or upgrade the seat.

## Graduated pricing (illustrative)

Billable seats are priced graduated (exact Stripe tiers apply at checkout):

1. Seat 1 → higher solo rate
2. Seats 2–7 → mid team rate each
3. Seats 8+ → lower scale rate each

Marketing illustrations:

| Example | Billable seats | Free support seats | Portal publishing (Rightmove / EG) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial Solo | 1 | 0 | Property Hive website sync only |
| Commercial Team | 2–7 (example card often uses 4) | 2 | Included |
| Commercial Scale | 8+ (example card often uses 10) | 4 | Included |

**Portal publishing** to Rightmove and EACH / EG requires **at least two billable seats**. Property Hive website sync remains available on Solo.

## Invite with the right seat

1. Open `/app/[account]/members`.
2. Invite the colleague.
3. Choose **billable** for negotiators and anyone who edits stock or stages.
4. Choose **support** for PAs, trainees, or viewers within your free support allowance.
5. Do not assign more billable members than your subscribed quantity without updating billing.
6. After accept, confirm their seat kind on the members list.

## Change seats and plan

1. Open workspace **Billing**.
2. Adjust billable seat quantity to match the negotiators you need.
3. Confirm the graduated monthly estimate.
4. Save — Stripe updates the subscription item quantity.
5. Invite or convert members so billable headcount matches seats purchased.
6. Recheck Website & portals unlock after moving from 1 → 2+ billable seats.

## Practical tips

1. Keep owners/admins billable during setup.
2. Use support seats for visibility without inflating Stripe quantity.
3. Unlock Rightmove / EG only when you intentionally take a second billable seat.
4. If publishing is blocked, count billable seats first — then credentials.
