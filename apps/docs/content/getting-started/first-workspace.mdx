# First workspace

Your personal account is created with you. Team workspaces are shared spaces for studios and agencies.

## What to configure first

- **Workspace name and branding** — so clients and teammates recognise the space
- **Timezone and working hours** — for planner and reminders
- **Modules you actually use** — pipeline, invoicing, activity, email assistant, and so on

You can change these later in workspace settings. See [Workspace](/workspace) for personal vs team account behaviour.
