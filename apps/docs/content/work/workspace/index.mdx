# Workspace

Ozer is multi-tenant: every client, project, and invoice belongs to an **account** (workspace). Business work runs inside a team account at `/app/[account]/…`.

## Goal

Understand how personal and Business accounts differ, and how the shell is organised so you can find features quickly.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in to Ozer
- At least one Business workspace ([First workspace](/work/getting-started/first-workspace))

## Personal vs Business at a glance

| | Personal | Business (team) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | You alone | Shared with members |
| Best for | Solo planner, life modules, private context | Studios and small agencies |
| Invites | N/A | Roles & permissions via **Team** |
| URL | `/app/…` | `/app/[account]/…` |

Switch workspaces from the **account switcher** at the top of the sidebar. The URL slug identifies the Business workspace.

## Steps to orient yourself

1. Open the account switcher and select your Business workspace.
2. Confirm the browser path starts with `/app/[account]/`.
3. Scan the sidebar sections: **Work**, **Clients**, **Commercial**, **Team & tools**, **Apps**.
4. Open **Settings** (`/app/[account]/settings`) for General, Brand, Billing, Payments, and Activity privacy.

## Tips

- Bookmark `/app/[account]/clients` or `/app/[account]/projects` after your slug stabilises.
- If a teammate can’t see a section you see, compare roles under **Team** and module toggles in Settings.

Continue to [Clients & pipeline](/work/clients-pipeline) once you know where things live.
