# Workspace

Ozer is multi-tenant: every disposal, contact, viewing, and proposal belongs to an **account** (workspace). Commercial property work runs on a team workspace with the commercial property profile.

## Personal vs team

| | Personal | Commercial team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | You alone | Shared with members |
| Best for | Hub across life and work | Agency disposals, WIP, portals |
| Invites | N/A | Roles, billable / support seats |
| URL | Personal home routes | `/app/[account]/…` |

## Steps to switch workspace

1. Open the account switcher in the app shell.
2. Select the commercial team workspace (not your personal hub).
3. Confirm the URL contains your slug: `/app/[account]/…`.
4. Check the sidebar shows commercial labels (Disposals, WIP, Contacts, Requirements).

## Key ideas

1. **Slug = account** — replace `[account]` with your workspace slug in every path in these docs.
2. **Seat kind** — billable members edit agency data; support seats are limited (see [Seats & billing](/commercial-property/seats-billing)).
3. **Modules** — workspace settings can show or hide nav items; missing keys default to on for new modules.
