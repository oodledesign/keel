# Invite your team

## Goal

Add colleagues to your Business workspace with roles that match what they should see and edit.

## Prerequisites

- A Business team workspace you own or administer
- Colleague email addresses
- Seats available on your plan (see [Workspace subscription](/work/invoicing-billing/workspace-subscription))

## Steps

1. Switch to the Business workspace with the account switcher.
2. In the sidebar under **Team & tools**, open **Team** (`/app/[account]/members`).
3. Choose invite / add member (also available at `/app/[account]/members/invites`).
4. Enter the email address and select a **role** (owner, admin, or member — exact labels depend on your workspace roles).
5. Send the invitation.
6. Ask your colleague to open the invite email (or the in-app invitation), accept it, and sign in with the same email.
7. Confirm they appear under **Team** and can open shared Clients and Projects.

## What you’ll see

- Member list with roles and status
- Pending invites until the person accepts
- Role-gated sidebar items (someone without invoice permission won’t see **Invoices**)

## Tips

- Invite owners/admins first for setup, then collaborators.
- Use the shared Business workspace for client work; keep purely personal items in your personal account when that split helps.
- Permissions follow workspace roles; Postgres row-level security enforces access on the server — you don’t configure RLS yourself.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Invite email missing | Check spam; resend from **Team**; confirm the email spelling |
| “Seat limit” or can’t invite | Upgrade plan or remove unused members under **Settings → Billing** |
| Member sees empty sidebar | They may need a different role, or modules are disabled for the workspace |
| Stale permissions after role change | Have them sign out and back in |
