# First Business workspace

## Goal

Create a shared Business (team) workspace with a clear name, slug, and the modules you’ll actually use.

## Prerequisites

- An authenticated Ozer account ([Create your account](/work/getting-started/create-account))
- Permission to create a team workspace (any signed-in user can start one)

## Personal vs Business

| | Personal (`/app`) | Business (`/app/[account]`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | You alone | Shared with members |
| Best for | Private planner, life tools, personal CRM | Studio CRM, jobs, invoices, portals |
| Invites | N/A for workspace sharing | **Team** + roles |

Your personal hub is created with you. Business workspaces are separate accounts with members, billing, and `/app/[account]/…` routes.

## Steps

1. From the account switcher (top of the sidebar), choose to create a new workspace / team account when offered, or complete workspace setup during onboarding.
2. Set a **workspace name** clients and teammates will recognise (for example your studio brand).
3. Confirm the **slug** — it becomes `[account]` in URLs such as `/app/[account]/clients`.
4. Pick the Business / work profile when asked so you get Clients, Pipeline, Projects, and Invoices rather than property or community modules.
5. Open **Settings** (footer of the sidebar or `/app/[account]/settings`) and check:
   - **General** — name, timezone, basics
   - **Brand** — logo and colours used on client-facing surfaces
   - Modules / features your plan enables (pipeline, invoicing, activity, websites, and so on)
6. Optionally set **Payments** and **Services** under Settings so invoices and proposals have sensible defaults.
7. Open **Dashboard** in the **Work** sidebar section to confirm you’re inside `/app/[account]`.

## What you’ll see in the sidebar

Once modules load, expect sections labelled **Work**, **Clients**, **Commercial**, and **Team & tools**. Missing items usually means a module is switched off or your role lacks access — see [Navigation](/work/workspace/navigation).

## Tips

- Choose a short, stable slug; changing it later breaks bookmarks and portal links that embed the path.
- Set timezone early — Planner and scheduling use it for “today”.
- Keep one Business workspace per brand or entity; create another account when you need a hard boundary.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Landed in personal `/app` only | Use the account switcher; create or select the Business workspace |
| Sidebar missing Clients / Invoices | Check Settings modules and your plan under [Workspace subscription](/work/invoicing-billing/workspace-subscription) |
| Wrong product profile | Confirm Business/work profile in Settings; commercial property is a different docs workspace |

More on account types: [Personal & team accounts](/work/workspace/accounts).
