# Brochures

Every disposal can have a PDF brochure built from its own details: the marketing copy, photos, floor plans, EPC, location and branch. You can then edit any page before you publish it. Building, editing, publishing and downloading a brochure uses **no AI credits**.

You need a **billable** seat to create or edit a brochure, as for editing disposals.

## Create a brochure

1. Open the disposal and go to **Publishing**.
2. Under **PDF brochure**, choose **Create brochure** (or **Review brochure** if one exists).
3. The brochure editor opens with four steps: **Set up**, **Review pages**, **Check the PDF** and **Approve**.

Before you start, check the disposal's **Marketing** copy and **Media**. The brochure fills in from them, so it is quicker to fix a description or upload a better photo there first.

## Set up

1. Pick an orientation. **Landscape** is the default; choose **Portrait** for traditional particulars. Each orientation keeps its own saved pages.
2. If pages are already saved for that orientation, choose:
   - **Continue where you left off** to keep your edited pages.
   - **Start fresh** to rebuild every page from the disposal. Your page edits are replaced; your brochure settings are kept.
3. Under **Show on the brochure**, switch off anything that should not appear, such as rent, price, size, business rates, service charge or estate charge. Rent and price start from the disposal's own POA settings on **Marketing**.
4. Choose **Build pages** (or **Continue**).

When you continue with saved pages, the rent, price and charges toggles are locked because those figures are already on your pages. Edit them on the pages, or start fresh to rebuild them.

## Pages filled from the disposal

A new brochure includes these pages, depending on what the disposal has:

| Page                  | What it shows                                                                                              |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cover                 | Hero photo, disposal badge, address, and size, use, tenure and rent or price                               |
| Summary               | Key facts table with photos                                                                                |
| Description           | Copy from Marketing with key points                                                                        |
| Specification & terms | Amenities, marketing sections and terms                                                                    |
| Photos                | Photos from Media, in several grid layouts                                                                 |
| Floor plan            | Floor plans from Media                                                                                     |
| Energy performance    | The EPC rating on an A+ to G scale beside the certificate, when an EPC is uploaded                         |
| Location              | Location copy, nearest stations, motorway junctions and towns with distances, and a map with numbered pins |
| Contact               | Office details, shopfront photo, agents and the important notice                                           |

Distances are straight-line, for guidance only. Agency logo and colours come from the workspace brand settings. Office details and the shopfront photo come from the disposal's branch.

## Review pages

The left column lists the pages. The middle shows a live preview of the selected page, and the right column has its fields.

### Arrange pages

1. Drag a page by its handle to reorder it.
2. Hover a page to **duplicate** or **delete** it.
3. Choose **Add** to insert a new page after the selected one. New pages are filled from the disposal. A brochure can have up to 30 pages.
4. Use **Page content** at the top to change what a page shows, and **Layout** to pick a different arrangement, for example a two-photo or four-photo grid. If you switch content and then switch back, your edits return.

### Edit text

1. Select a page and edit its heading, body, key points or facts on the right.
2. Key points take one point per line.
3. On the summary page, add or remove fact rows as needed.

### Listing data in text

Text fields can show live listing data, such as the rent or size, so the brochure stays right when the disposal changes.

1. Click the **braces** button `{ }` at the top-right of any text field.
2. Choose the detail to insert, for example **Rent**, **Size**, **Town** or **EPC**. It appears as a small coloured pill where your cursor was.
3. You can also type the name in braces, for example `{rent}`, and it turns into a pill.

The PDF shows the disposal's current value in place of each pill. Rent and price follow the **Show on the brochure** toggles and the disposal's POA settings. Text in braces that is not a known detail stays as you typed it.

Available details: Disposal, Rent, Price, Size, Tenure, Use class, Business rates, Service charge, Estate charge, EPC, Available from, Property name, Address, Town and Postcode.

### Images

1. The media tray under the preview shows every photo, floor plan and EPC on the disposal. Labels show whether each image is **In use** or **Unused**.
2. Drag an image onto any image space, or select a space and click an image in the tray.
3. For each image, choose how it fits: **Auto**, **Fill frame** or **Whole image**. Auto shows floor plans and drawings whole, and lets photos fill the frame.
4. When a photo fills the frame, choose which part to keep in view: **Top**, **Centre** or **Bottom**.

To add new images, upload them on the disposal's **Media** tab and reopen the brochure.

### Location map

On a location page, use **Places to show** to show all nearby places, up to 8, 5 or 3, or none (property pin only). You can rename places, change their distance or icon, and remove ones you do not want.

## Brochure settings

**Brochure settings** is pinned at the bottom of the page list. Its options apply to every page. While it is open, the preview shows a page with the footer so you can see your changes.

### Footer

| Setting        | What it does                                                                                     |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Office details | Shows the agency, branch address and phone. Type your own text to replace it, or leave it blank. |
| Notice         | Shows **Subject to contract** by default. Type your own wording to replace it.                   |
| Page numbers   | Shows the page number on each page.                                                              |

Switch any of them off to remove it from the footer. The office details and notice fields accept listing data pills too, for example `{Town}`. The cover page never has a footer.

### Buttons and badges

1. **Reduced price badge** adds a **Reduced price** badge to the cover. Switch it on only when the price has come down.
2. **Website listing button** adds a link to the disposal on your website.
3. **Online brochure button** adds a link to the online brochure.

Buttons only appear when the disposal has that link. To hide a button on one page only, use **Buttons on this page** in that page's fields.

## Check the PDF

1. Choose **Check the PDF** to build the full PDF.
2. Step through each page. The **Checks** panel lists anything that needs a look:
   - an image is low resolution and may look blurry when printed
   - an image space is empty
   - some text did not fit and was cut short
   - a page has nothing on it, so it was left out
   - some nearby places were left out of a crowded map
3. Choose **Show** to jump to the page, or **Fix** (or **Fix this page**) to go straight back to editing it.

Even with no problems found, look through every page before you approve.

## Approve and publish

1. Choose **Approve**.
2. Tick **I've checked every page of the PDF**.
3. Choose **Download PDF** for a copy, or **Publish**.
4. Confirm. The dialog lists the feeds the brochure will be sent to.

Publishing saves the PDF to the disposal's media and replaces any brochure published before, including one in the other orientation. Only a published brochure is sent to your website and portals. If no feeds are switched on yet, the brochure is saved and sent once a feed is on.

## After publishing

Your edits save automatically as you work. The **PDF brochure** panel on **Publishing** shows where the brochure stands:

| Status                  | Meaning                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Not created             | No brochure yet.                                                                    |
| Draft                   | Pages are saved but not published. Feeds do not have it.                            |
| Published               | The date and who published it.                                                      |
| Edited since publishing | Pages or settings changed after publishing. Feeds still have the published version. |

Use **Download draft** to get a PDF of unpublished changes. To update the feeds, choose **Review brochure**, check the PDF and publish again.

Have your own designed PDF instead? Upload it on **Media → Brochure**.
