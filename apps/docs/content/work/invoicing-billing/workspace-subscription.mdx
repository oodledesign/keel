# Workspace subscription

## Goal

Choose and manage the Ozer Business plan (and add-ons) that funds this team workspace.

## Prerequisites

- Business team account
- Permission to view billing (`Settings → Billing`)
- Payment method ready for checkout (Stripe)

This is **not** client invoicing. Client bills live under [Client invoices](/work/invoicing-billing/client-invoices).

## Business plans (summary)

Exact prices can change — confirm on [ozer.so/pricing](https://www.ozer.so/pricing). Typical Business products:

| Plan | Best for | Typical inclusions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** (Business Lite) | Light use | 2 seats; 3 clients; 5 invoices/month; 20 open tasks; 5 bookings/month; 250 MB portal; meeting recording 5 hrs/mo. No planner, email assistant, or coaching. Add-ons not bundled. |
| **Starter** | Solo freelancers | Unlimited clients, invoices, tasks, scheduling; 10 GB portal; recording unlimited. £14 seat 1, then £9 each extra. No planner, email assistant, or coaching. |
| **Pro** | Studios | Unlimited ops; 25 GB; recording unlimited; planner, email assistant, coaching, and auto tasks; second brain indexes transcripts. £29 seat 1, then £22 each extra. Shared AI pool scales with seats. |

Trials (where offered) are commonly 14 days on paid Business plans. Annual billing on Pro is 10× monthly (16.7% less than 12×). 1 / 4 / 10 seat figures on marketing pages are examples on Starter or Pro — not separate products.

Common **add-ons** (when sold separately): branded signatures, media units, and similar marketplace apps. Email assistant is included on Pro and spends the workspace AI credit pool.

## Steps: start or change a plan

1. Switch to the Business workspace you want billed.
2. Open **Settings → Billing** → `/app/[account]/settings/billing`.
3. Review current plan, seats, and renewal.
4. Choose **Free**, **Starter**, or **Pro** as appropriate, then set billable seats.
5. Complete Stripe Checkout; you’ll return via the billing return route (`/app/[account]/billing/return`).
6. Confirm the new plan features unlock (sidebar modules, seat capacity).

## Steps: manage payment method and invoices from Ozer

1. From **Settings → Billing**, open the Stripe customer portal when offered (update card, download Ozer receipts).
2. Adjust seats on the same Starter or Pro product — extra seats stay cheaper than seat 1.
3. Cancel or change renewal only from Billing / portal — cancelling does not delete workspace data immediately, but modules may lock when entitlement ends.

## What you’ll see

- Plan cards and feature lists
- Current subscription status and trial countdown when applicable
- Add-on toggles or marketplace upsells for Email assistant, portals, etc.

## Tips

- Bill the correct workspace — each Business account has its own subscription.
- Invite seats only after the plan supports them ([Invite your team](/work/getting-started/invite-team)).
- For marketing comparisons see [Pricing](https://www.ozer.so/pricing).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Billing page missing | You need billing permission; ask an owner |
| Checkout succeeded but features missing | Refresh; wait a moment for webhooks; sign out/in |
| Seat invite blocked | Add seats on Starter or Pro, or remove inactive members |
| Confused with client invoices | Use **Commercial → Invoices** for client billing |
