Ozer extension

Minimal permissions. Meet names come from the page you already see. No WebRTC tap, no ASR in the browser.

Canonical: http://127.0.0.1:18791 — POST /v1/meet/speaker-stamps. Older 17834 builds still work as an alias.

Shortcut: Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + O. Change it in chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
