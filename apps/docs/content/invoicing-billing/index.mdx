# Invoicing & billing

Two related ideas:

1. **Client invoices** — bill your clients from Ozer (create, send, track).
2. **Ozer subscription** — your workspace plan on Stripe (see [Pricing](https://www.ozer.so/pricing)).

## Client invoicing

Create invoices against clients and projects, send them, and follow status through to paid. Integrations with tools like FreeAgent may appear under finances depending on your workspace setup.

## Workspace subscription

Manage plan, seats, and payment method from **Billing** in the app. Flat team pricing details are on the marketing site.
