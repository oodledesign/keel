# Security & trust

Ozer is built for people who care where their data lives. Personal is private to you; Business and other workspaces keep their own tenancy boundaries.

## At a glance

1. Read the marketing **Trust** overview: [ozer.so/trust](https://www.ozer.so/trust).
2. Know that workspace data access is enforced with Postgres **row-level security** — you see what your ownership or membership allows.
3. Auth and billing use established providers (for example Supabase Auth and Stripe).
4. Keep Personal free of sensitive client CRM if that data belongs in a controlled Business workspace.

## Personal account practices

1. Open **Personal settings** (`/app/settings`) and review preferences, accessibility, and integrations.
2. Connect only the personal Gmail you intend for **Personal email** (`/app/email`).
3. Sign out on shared devices and use a strong unique password or OAuth provider you trust.
4. Use Support in-product, or email [hello@ozer.so](mailto:hello@ozer.so), if something looks wrong with access.

## Where business data should live

Clients, pipeline, invoices, activity, and portals stay in [Business](/work). Personal may surface tasks for planning; it is not the system of record for studio CRM.
