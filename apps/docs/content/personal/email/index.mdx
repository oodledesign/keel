# Personal email

**Personal email** (`/app/email`) connects your personal Gmail mailbox (where available) for triage, suggested to-dos, and AI draft replies in your voice.

This is the Personal mailbox — not Business email for studio inboxes. Studio email assistant behaviour for team accounts is covered under [Business](/work).

## Connect and open

1. Open **Personal email** in the sidebar (`/app/email`).
2. Connect Gmail when prompted, and approve the access Ozer needs for inbox assist.
3. Confirm the mailbox shows as personal; reconnect from **Personal settings** → integrations (`/app/settings/integrations`) if the link drops.
4. Return to `/app/email` to triage.

## Triage the inbox

1. Scan messages that need a reply; skip noise you have ignored by sender or domain.
2. Open a thread and review any draft suggestion before sending.
3. Adjust style notes or signature in settings so drafts stay in your voice.
4. Mark handled mail and move on — keep the hub calm.

## Suggested tasks

1. Open suggested to-dos at `/app/email/suggested-tasks` when the assistant surfaces follow-ups.
2. Accept useful suggestions into **Tasks** (`/app/tasks`).
3. Dismiss suggestions that are not actionable.
4. Schedule accepted work in **Planner** (`/app/planner/plan`) if it belongs today.

## Tips

- Personal email is for your life inbox; client and studio mail belongs with Business tooling.
- Prefer reviewing drafts before auto-save behaviours send anything you did not intend.
- Availability of Gmail connect and AI assist can depend on plan and region — see [Pricing](https://www.ozer.so/pricing) and [Security & trust](/personal/security-trust).
