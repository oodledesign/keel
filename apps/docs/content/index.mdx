# Introduction

Ozer documentation is organised by **workspace type**. Pick the kind of workspace you use — the sidebar and guides will only show what’s relevant for that product.

Use the **Workspace** dropdown at the top of the sidebar at any time to switch.

## Choose your workspace

| Workspace | Best for | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Personal](/personal) | Your free hub — home, tasks, planner, notes, people, and personal email | [Personal overview](/personal) |
| [Business](/work) | Studio / agency CRM — clients, pipeline, projects, invoices, portals | [Business overview](/work) |
| [Commercial property](/commercial-property) | Agency disposals, WIP, requirements, viewings, HoTs, and portal publishing | [Property overview](/commercial-property) |

## How docs are organised

1. Open this site (or follow a product **Help** link).
2. Choose a workspace in the sidebar dropdown (or use a card above).
3. Follow **Getting started**, then open feature guides from the sidebar as you need them.

App URLs in the guides use:

- **Personal** — `/app/...` (no account slug)
- **Business** and **Commercial property** — `/app/[account]/...` where `[account]` is your team workspace slug
