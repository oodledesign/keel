# Introduction

Ozer is the **Workspace OS** for freelancers and small studios. Clients, projects, invoices, pipeline, activity tracking, and your plan for the day live in one place — instead of seven tools glued together with Zapier.

This documentation covers how to set up your workspace and use the core product areas.

## Who it’s for

- Freelancers who want CRM, jobs, and invoicing without enterprise overhead
- Small agencies and studios that need a shared home for work and life
- Teams that care about flat pricing and data hosted in the EU
