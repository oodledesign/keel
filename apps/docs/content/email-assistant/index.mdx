# Email assistant

Connect Gmail (where available) to triage inbox mail, draft replies in your voice, and skip noise with ignored senders/domains.

## Highlights

- Auto-triage and draft suggestions
- Optional auto-save of drafts to Gmail
- Settings for style notes and signature

Marketing: [Email assistant](https://www.ozer.so/features/email-assistant).
