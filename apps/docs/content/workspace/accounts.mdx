# Personal & team accounts

- **Personal account** — linked to your user ID; good for private pipelines and life/work context that isn’t shared.
- **Team account** — a workspace with members, roles, and shared CRM/projects/invoices.

Data is scoped by `account_id`. You only see what your membership (or ownership) allows.

Create additional team workspaces when you need a clean boundary between brands or entities.
