# Contacts

Contacts are the commercial CRM records for people and organisations you work with. Open them at `/app/[account]/clients` (sidebar label: **Contacts**).

## Contact roles

Common commercial roles include:

1. **Landlord** — owner / disposing party
2. **Tenant** — occupational occupier / applicant
3. **Investor** — investment buyer or seller side
4. **Solicitor** — legal counsel on a deal
5. **Agent** — co-agent or introducing agent

## Create a contact

1. Go to `/app/[account]/clients`.
2. Choose **New contact** (or the create action on the page).
3. Enter name, company, email, phone, and role.
4. Save the record.
5. Link the contact from disposals (Management parties), requirements, viewings, or interest as needed.

## Use contacts across the desk

1. On a disposal’s **Management** tab, attach landlords and other parties from Contacts.
2. When logging a requirement, select the applicant contact first.
3. When booking a viewing, choose the attendee contacts and the disposal.
4. Keep solicitor details on the contact so HoTs / Proposals can reference the right people.
5. Prefer updating one canonical contact over duplicating records for the same person.

## Housekeeping

1. Merge or rename duplicates when two negotiators create the same contact.
2. Note preferred communication channel (email / phone) in the contact notes.
3. Respect support vs billable limits: editing contacts may require a billable seat depending on your workspace permissions.
