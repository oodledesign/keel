# Circulation & email compliance

This is an **internal product brief** for Ozer Commercial property. It is **not legal advice**. Agencies should have UK privacy counsel review soft opt-in copy and their own privacy notices.

## What “circulation” is

1. Occupiers / investors register a **requirement** (website embed or CRM).
2. Agents match a disposal to active requirements.
3. Agents **circulate** particulars by email to that matching set.
4. Recipients can **unsubscribe** from matching-opportunity emails for that agency.

## Email classification


| Message | Class | Pipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Thanks — we received your requirement” | Transactional / service | ZeptoMail |
| One-to-one reply about a specific enquiry / listing | Transactional / service | ZeptoMail |
| “New letting that may match your registered requirement” (list) | **Marketing** (solicited / soft opt-in or LI) | **Amazon SES** |
| General newsletter / market commentary | Marketing (usually consent) | Amazon SES |

Having a lawful basis does **not** make a circulation blast transactional. ZeptoMail’s terms forbid marketing/bulk; do not send circulation through Zepto.

## Lawful basis (UK B2B PECR + UK GDPR)

For **B2B** commercial applicants, PECR is more permissive than consumer marketing, but you still need:

1. **Clear sender identity** — agency brand From / body (not “via Ozer” alone).
2. **Easy opt-out** on every circulation email (unsubscribe link + honour promptly).
3. A documented basis, typically:
   - **Soft opt-in / solicited:** they submitted the requirement form asking to be notified of matching opportunities; or
   - **Legitimate interests** + balancing test for existing business contacts (use the platform LIA template / agency LIA); or
   - **Consent** where soft opt-in does not apply (cold, bought, or residential).

**Not permission:** “their email is already in the CRM.”

### Suggested soft opt-in notice (B2B — counsel to approve)

> By submitting this form you ask {Agency} to store your requirement and email you commercial property opportunities that may match. You can unsubscribe at any time. See our [privacy notice]({url}).

### Purpose limitation

Circulation emails should only promote **matching disposals** for the purpose `matching_disposals`. Do not silently expand to newsletters without a separate preference / consent.

## Roles

| Party | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Agency (workspace) | **Controller** of applicant data and circulation content |
| Ozer | **Processor** hosting CRM, embed, and SES send on the agency’s behalf |
| Amazon SES | Sub-processor for marketing/circulation delivery |
| Zoho ZeptoMail | Sub-processor for **transactional** product email only |

Update the DPA / data-processing inventory when enabling circulation: list SES for marketing sends; do not imply Zepto covers blasts.

## Product rules (enforced in Ozer)

1. Upserting a requirement **must not** re-subscribe someone who unsubscribed.
2. Circulation recipients are filtered to `marketing_status = subscribed` (and not suppressed).
3. Imported historical lists default to `lawful_basis = imported_historical` and should not circulate until the agency attests or re-permissions.
4. Every SES circulation message includes `List-Unsubscribe` and a branded unsubscribe page scoped to that workspace.
5. Agents preview the recipient set and confirm before send (audit log stores who/what/when).

## SES production access

Amazon SES requires production access for unrestricted sending. Re-apply with:

- Public company/product website (not behind auth or bot blocks)
- Use case text: *UK B2B commercial property applicants who registered requirements; opt-out on every email; no purchased lists*
- Live privacy policy + unsubscribe URL
- Verified sending domain (SPF/DKIM)
