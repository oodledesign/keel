# Clients & pipeline

Track leads and clients in one CRM that connects to projects, invoices, portals, and meetings.

## Goal

Build a reliable client list and move opportunities through your pipeline without switching tools.

## Prerequisites

- Business workspace at `/app/[account]`
- Permission to view clients / dashboard
- **Clients** and **Pipeline** modules enabled (sidebar under **Clients**)

## What lives where

| Surface | Sidebar | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client records | **Clients** | `/app/[account]/clients` | Companies, contacts, portal invites |
| Deal board | **Pipeline** | `/app/[account]/pipeline` | Stages from lead to won / lost |

## Guides in this section

- [Clients](/work/clients-pipeline/clients) — create, edit, import, link work
- [Pipeline](/work/clients-pipeline/pipeline) — stages, cards, converting to projects

## Typical first day

1. Import or create two or three active clients.
2. Open **Pipeline** and place open deals in the right stage.
3. From a client or won deal, create a **Project** (see [Projects](/work/projects-tasks/projects)).
4. When you bill, create an invoice linked to that client ([Client invoices](/work/invoicing-billing/client-invoices)).

## Tips

- Treat **Clients** as the system of record for people and companies; treat **Pipeline** as the sales motion.
- Portal access is driven from client contacts — see [Portals & websites](/work/portals-websites).
