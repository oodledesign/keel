# Workspace

Ozer is multi-tenant: every client, project, and invoice belongs to an **account** (workspace).

## Personal vs team

|           | Personal                   | Team                       |
| --------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Ownership | You alone                  | Shared with members        |
| Best for  | Solo freelancing, life CRM | Studios and small agencies |
| Invites   | N/A                        | Roles & permissions        |

Switch workspaces from the account switcher in the shell. The URL slug identifies the team workspace under `/app/[account]/…`.

See [Personal & team accounts](/workspace/accounts) and [Navigation](/workspace/navigation).
