# Getting started

Get from signup to a usable Business workspace in a few minutes.

## Goal

Create your Ozer account, open a Business (team) workspace, and optionally invite teammates so everyone shares the same clients and projects.

## Prerequisites

- A work email you can access for verification
- Browser access to [app.ozer.so](https://app.ozer.so) (or your local app host)

## Steps

1. [Create your account](/work/getting-started/create-account)
2. [Set up your first Business workspace](/work/getting-started/first-workspace)
3. [Invite teammates](/work/getting-started/invite-team) (optional)

## What you’ll see

After onboarding you’ll land in the workspace shell. Switch accounts from the account switcher at the top of the sidebar. Business workspaces use URLs like `/app/[account]/…`.
