# Invite your team

On a commercial team workspace, invite members from **Team** at `/app/[account]/members`.

## Steps

1. Open `/app/[account]/members`.
2. Choose **Invite** (or the equivalent invite action).
3. Enter the colleague’s email address.
4. Pick a workspace role appropriate to their job (owner/admin for setup; member for day-to-day agency work).
5. Choose a **seat kind**:
   - **Billable** — can create and edit disposals, move WIP/requirement stages, and configure publishing (subject to plan limits).
   - **Support** — can view and add notes/activity; cannot mutate stages, disposals, or publishing.
6. Send the invitation and ask them to accept from their email.
7. After they join, ask them to sign out and back in if menus look stale.

## Tips

1. Start with billable owners/admins for setup, then invite negotiators as billable seats.
2. Use free **support** seats for PAs, trainees, or viewers within your plan’s allowance.
3. Portal publishing (Rightmove / EACH / EG) needs at least **two billable seats** — see [Seats & billing](/commercial-property/seats-billing).
4. Keep personal life CRM items in your personal account; agency contacts and instructions stay on the commercial workspace.

Detailed seat pricing and allowances: [Seats & billing](/commercial-property/seats-billing).
