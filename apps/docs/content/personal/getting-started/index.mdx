# Getting started

Get from signup to a usable Personal workspace in a few minutes. Personal is free and created with you — no separate “create Personal” step after signup.

## Steps

1. [Create your account](/personal/getting-started/create-account)
2. [Find your way around Personal home](/personal/getting-started/personal-home)
3. Optionally open or create a [Business workspace](/work) when you need clients, pipeline, and invoicing

## After setup

- Pin the surfaces you open most from [Home hub](/personal/home-hub)
- Add a few [tasks](/personal/tasks-planner/tasks) or open [Planner](/personal/tasks-planner/planner)
- Keep friends and catch-ups in [People](/personal/people) — not a sales CRM

Need help? Use **Support** in the product, or email [hello@ozer.so](mailto:hello@ozer.so).
