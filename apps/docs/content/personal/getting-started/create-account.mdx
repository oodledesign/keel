# Create your account

Sign up at [app.ozer.so](https://app.ozer.so) (or your local app host) with email and password, a magic link, or a supported OAuth provider.

## Steps

1. Open the sign-up screen and choose your method (email, magic link, or OAuth).
2. Confirm your email if prompted.
3. Complete the short onboarding flow — your **Personal** account is created with you.
4. When setup finishes, you land in Personal home at `/app`.

## What you get immediately

- Personal sidebar: **Home**, **Tasks**, **Projects**, **Planner**, **Personal email**, **Notes**, **People**
- **Personal settings** in the sidebar footer (`/app/settings`)
- The workspace switcher (when team workspaces are enabled) so you can open Business or other spaces later

## What Personal is not

Personal is the free hub, not a full CRM. Clients, pipeline, invoicing, activity tracking, and client portals live in a [Business workspace](/work). You can start in Personal first and add Business when you need them.
