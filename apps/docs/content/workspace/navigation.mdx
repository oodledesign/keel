# Navigation

The workspace shell groups product areas in the sidebar:

- **Home / planner** — plan for the day
- **Pipeline & clients** — CRM
- **Projects & tasks** — delivery
- **Invoices & finances** — getting paid
- **Activity, meetings, email** — how work actually happened

Marketing site: [Features](https://www.ozer.so/features). Apps overview: [Apps](https://www.ozer.so/apps).
