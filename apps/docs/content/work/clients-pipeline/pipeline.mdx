# Pipeline

## Goal

Move opportunities through named stages so the team sees what’s active, stuck, or won — and can spin up projects when deals close.

## Prerequisites

- Business workspace with **Pipeline** visible under the **Clients** sidebar section
- Preferably a few [Clients](/work/clients-pipeline/clients) already created

## Steps: open and read the board

1. Click **Pipeline** in the sidebar → `/app/[account]/pipeline`.
2. Scan columns (stages). Cards represent deals / opportunities tied to clients.
3. Use filters or search on the board if your workspace exposes them, to focus on owner or stage.

## Steps: create and progress a deal

1. On **Pipeline**, create a new card / opportunity.
2. Link it to an existing **client** (create the client first if needed).
3. Set title, value, and owner when those fields are available.
4. Drag the card between stages as the deal progresses (for example Lead → Proposal → Won).
5. When the deal is won, create a **Project** from the opportunity or client so delivery continues under **Projects** (`/app/[account]/projects`).

## Steps: keep pipeline honest

1. Archive or move lost deals to a Lost stage instead of deleting history you may need.
2. Review the board weekly in Planner downtime so stale cards don’t linger.
3. Connect won work to invoices under **Invoices** once delivery is billable.

## What you’ll see

- Kanban-style columns labelled by stage
- Cards showing client / deal summary
- Empty columns until you add opportunities

## Tips

- Align stage names with how your studio actually sells (your workspace may allow stage customisation in settings or board config).
- One active primary opportunity per major engagement keeps reporting cleaner.
- Proposals and contracts under **Commercial** often sit alongside late pipeline stages — open **Proposals** / **Contracts** when you need formal documents.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Pipeline missing from sidebar | Enable the pipeline module or check your role |
| Can’t drag cards | Confirm you have edit access; refresh if the board is stale |
| No clients to link | Create clients first under **Clients** |
