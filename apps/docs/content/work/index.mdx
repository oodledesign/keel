# Ozer Business

Ozer Business is the team workspace for freelancers and small studios. Clients, pipeline, projects, invoices, activity, email, and client portals live under one account slug at `/app/[account]/…`.

This documentation walks through the Business product — not Personal life tools or Commercial property agency workflows. Use the **Workspace** switcher in the docs navbar to move between documentation areas.

## Who it’s for

- Solo freelancers billing clients and tracking deals
- Small agencies and studios sharing one CRM and delivery board
- Teams that want flat pricing, EU-hosted data, and client-facing portals

## What you’ll see in the app sidebar

In a Business workspace the left sidebar is grouped roughly as:

- **Work** — Dashboard, Projects, Tasks, Planner, Schedule, Scheduling
- **Clients** — Pipeline, Clients, Meetings, Activity, Websites, Support (Emails appears here when the email add-on is available)
- **Commercial** — Invoices, Proposals, Contracts, Finances
- **Team & tools** — Team, Notes and files, Second brain, SOPs, Messages
- **Apps** — optional marketplace apps (Rankly, Signatures, and so on)

Exact labels depend on modules enabled under **Settings**. Paths in these guides use `/app/[account]/…` where `[account]` is your workspace slug.
