# Create your account

Sign up at [app.ozer.so](https://app.ozer.so) (or your local app host) with email and password, magic link, or a supported OAuth provider.

## Steps

1. Open the app signup page and choose email, magic link, or OAuth.
2. Enter your details and confirm your email if prompted.
3. When asked for workspace type / profile, choose **Commercial property** (not Business / studio).
4. Complete the short onboarding prompts for name, agency identity, and timezone.
5. Land in your personal hub, then open or create the team workspace you will use for agency data.

## After signup

- Your personal account is created automatically
- Commercial agency work belongs on a **team workspace** with the commercial property profile
- Confirm email before inviting colleagues if the product asks you to

Need help during onboarding? Use **Support** in the product, or email [hello@ozer.so](mailto:hello@ozer.so).
