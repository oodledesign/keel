# WIP pipeline

WIP (work in progress) is the commercial **instructions** board. Open it at `/app/[account]/pipeline`. The default board name is **WIP**; your workspace may rename it.

WIP tracks instruction stages for disposals — separate from the acquisition [Requirements](/commercial-property/requirements) board (same pipeline page, `?view=requirements`).

## Instruction stages

1. **Potential Instructions** — early / anticipated instructions
2. **Current Instructions** — live instructed work (often linked to marketing disposals)
3. **Under Offer / Negotiating** — heads of terms and negotiation
4. **Completed / Exchanged** — won / completed instructions
5. **Fallen through** — lost instructions

## Open WIP

1. Go to `/app/[account]/pipeline`.
2. Confirm you are on the WIP / instructions view (not Requirements).
3. Review columns left to right from Potential through Completed / Fallen through.
4. Drag a card between columns only if you are on a **billable** seat.

## Keep WIP and disposals aligned

1. Create or update the disposal on `/app/[account]/listings`.
2. When terms of engagement are agreed, move WIP to **Current Instructions** and disposal status toward **Instructed** / **Marketing**.
3. When offers land, move WIP to **Under Offer / Negotiating** and progress the disposal / interest schedule.
4. On exchange or completion, move WIP to **Completed / Exchanged** and set disposal status to **Let** or **Sold**.
5. If the deal dies, move WIP to **Fallen through** and set the disposal to **Withdrawn** when appropriate.

## Daily WIP routine

1. Open Insights at `/app/[account]/commercial-reports` for a quick health check.
2. Scan **Potential** for stale cards older than your desk’s chase window.
3. Scan **Current** against marketing disposals so nothing is live without an instruction card.
4. Triage **Under Offer / Negotiating** against HoTs / Proposals at `/app/[account]/proposals`.
5. Leave **Fallen through** visible long enough for reporting, then archive per your office policy.

## Requirements view

1. Switch to Requirements with `/app/[account]/pipeline?view=requirements` (also linked from the sidebar as **Requirements**).
2. Follow the [Requirements](/commercial-property/requirements) guide for stage meanings and matching to disposals.

Support seats cannot move stages — ask a billable colleague, or upgrade the seat ([Seats & billing](/commercial-property/seats-billing)).
