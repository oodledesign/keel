# Personal workspace

Personal is the free hub in Ozer. It connects life and every other workspace you join — so today, tasks, and shortcuts stay in one place without turning Personal into a full CRM.

URLs in Personal use `/app` with **no account slug** — for example `/app/tasks`, `/app/planner/plan`, and `/app/settings`.

## What Personal is for

- A private home for your day: tasks, planner, notes, people, and personal email
- Cross-workspace visibility so work from Business (and other spaces) can surface on Home and in Tasks when you want it
- A lightweight life context — not clients, pipeline, invoices, or client portals

Business features (clients, pipeline, invoicing, activity, and portals) live in a [Business workspace](/work).
