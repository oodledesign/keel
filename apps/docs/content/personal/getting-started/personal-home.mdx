# Personal home

Personal home (`/app`) is your private landing place after login. Use it to see what’s due, jump to shortcuts, and switch into other workspaces without losing the day.

## Steps

1. Open **Home** in the Personal sidebar (or go to `/app`).
2. Scan today’s highlights and anything due from Personal and, when enabled, other workspaces.
3. Use the account switcher at the top of the sidebar to open a Business or team workspace — or stay on Personal for life context.
4. Open **Personal settings** (`/app/settings`) to set timezone, preferences, and integrations you care about.

## Paths to remember

| Where | Path |
| ----- | ---- |
| Home | `/app` |
| Today (day view) | `/app/planner/day` |
| Planner | `/app/planner/plan` |
| Tasks | `/app/tasks` |
| Settings | `/app/settings` |

## Tips

- Personal URLs never include an account slug — team spaces do (`/app/[account]/…`).
- Prefer Business for client delivery; keep DIY, holidays, notes, and catch-ups in Personal.
- Cross-workspace task visibility is controlled from preferences tied to Personal — see [Home hub](/personal/home-hub).
